2. Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, phone, business name (if any), saved addresses, notification preferences.
  • Order details: pickup/drop-off addresses, receiver contact, package description/weight, payment status (we never collect card numbers — there is no card payment here).
  • Messages you send via forms or WhatsApp.

Delivery alerts

To keep you informed we send email and SMS alerts about order status (placed, confirmed, paid, picked up, in transit, out for delivery, delivered, cancelled) to the sender contacts provided, and to the receiver if their email/phone is supplied. Emails are sent via our mail service and SMS via our Nigerian SMS provider (Termii) solely to deliver these updates. Signed-in customers can manage alert channels in Profile Settings. Standard SMS/email delivery depends on networks.

Live rider location sharing

While a delivery is In Transit or Out for Delivery, your rider may share live GPS location from their phone so you can follow the trip. This location is only visible to you (via your Order ID/tracking link), the receiver you share the link with, and authorised FL Logistics staff. It is deleted when the order is delivered and is never sold. Riders choose to start/stop sharing per trip. Anyone with your tracking link can view the delivery status — share it only with people you trust.

How we use & share data

  • To pick up, route, deliver, communicate and support your orders; to prevent fraud; and to improve our service.
  • Your rider receives the pickup/drop-off details and phone numbers needed for the job.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (secure hosting/database/login), our SMS provider, email service, and maps providers (OpenStreetMap/Google Maps when you open directions).
  • We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose data where required by Nigerian law.

Storage, security & your rights

  • Data is stored in access-controlled cloud systems. No system is 100% secure — please use a strong password.
  • You may view/edit your profile and saved addresses in your dashboard, and delete your account there. Operational order records may be retained for business, tax and dispute purposes (EDIT retention period).
  • For privacy requests, contact us via the Contact page or WhatsApp 0813 740 7197. We aim to honour rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Cookies/local storage

We use essential login storage (Firebase) and remember recent Order IDs on your device for quick tracking. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site (EDIT if you add analytics).

3. Shipping & Prohibited Items Policy

Packaging

Pack items securely for road transport: cushion fragile goods, seal liquids in leak-proof containers (where accepted), and label "Fragile/This side up" clearly. We may re-pack unsafe parcels at an agreed fee or decline them.

Prohibited items

    Also prohibited: anything illegal to possess/transport under Nigerian law, items that endanger our riders or other parcels, and live/dead animals or human remains. Prohibited items may be handed to authorities and the booking cancelled without refund.

    Restricted (ask first)

    High-value electronics, glass, liquids, perishable food (needs cold packaging), medicines, and cash-equivalents require prior agreement and may need insurance. When in doubt, message us a photo of the item first.

    Weight & size

    Charges use actual or volumetric weight (size-based), whichever is higher. Oversized items may require a car/van and a custom Quote.
