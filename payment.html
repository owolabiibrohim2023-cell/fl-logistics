1. Book online

2. Confirm on WhatsApp

3. Make payment

4. Send proof

Send your transfer receipt/screenshot in the same WhatsApp chat. We mark your order Paid.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

Your receiver pays the rider in cash when the parcel arrives. Please tell your receiver the exact confirmed amount in advance so delivery is smooth. COD is agreed with you on WhatsApp before dispatch — some routes/items may require part-payment first.

Pay on Pickup

Pay our rider in cash (or instant transfer) when they collect the parcel from you. The rider confirms receipt to the office, and your order is marked Paid.

Payment status meanings

Payment: Pending

We've not confirmed payment yet. If you just transferred, send the receipt on WhatsApp and give us a few minutes to verify.

Payment: Paid

Payment received and confirmed. You'll also get an automatic SMS/email alert when we mark it Paid.

Payment: COD

Cash on delivery agreed. The receiver will pay the rider the confirmed amount at the door.
