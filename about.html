Our Story

From Ogba, for all of Nigeria

FL Logistics started at Ste 48, Ogba Shopping Arcade (LSDPC), Ijaiye Road, Ogba, Lagos with a bike, a phone, and a stubborn belief: delivery in Lagos can be fast and trustworthy.

Today, online vendors, offices, pharmacies and families across Lagos trust us daily, and our interstate runs carry parcels safely to Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Enugu, Kano and beyond. We've grown, but the habit hasn't changed: clear communication, honest pricing confirmed before payment, and riders who pick up the phone.

With online booking, live GPS tracking and automatic SMS/email alerts, you now see what we see — your parcel's journey, step by step, until it reaches its final destination.
